Data and Supplementary Materials for PhD Thesis

This repository contains the data, analysis files, computational models, and supplementary materials associated with the PhD thesis "Force, Movement, and Fracture: A Biomechanical Investigation of Stone Tool Production" by Cecilia Barroso Medina, University of Wollongong.

The repository is organised by thesis chapter. Each chapter folder contains the data associated with the experiments and analyses presented in that chapter. File names have been retained or modified to provide a clear correspondence between the repository contents and the analyses described in the thesis.

Repository structure
Chapter 3 – Design and development of a sensorized hammerstone for accurate force measurement in stone knapping experiments

This folder contains the data and three-dimensional models associated with the design, development, calibration, and experimental validation of the sensorized hammerstone described in Chapter 3.

3D models

The .stl files contain the individual components used during the development of the sensorized hammerstone and associated experimental apparatus.

Chamber_updated.stl – 3D model of the updated pressure chamber.
Grip_steel_hammerstone.stl – grip component of the steel sensorized hammerstone.
Gripping 3d geometry.stl – 3D geometry associated with the hammerstone gripping component.
Platform.stl – experimental platform component.
Pneumatic_chamber.stl – 3D model of the pneumatic chamber.
Sensor_shell_enclosure.stl – enclosure for the sensor assembly.
Sensor_shell_lid.stl – lid of the sensor enclosure.
Striker_part.stl – striker component.
Striker_steel_hammerstone.stl – striker component of the steel sensorized hammerstone.
Calibration and experimental data

The .txt files contain the pressure–force calibration data and force–mass data generated during the development and validation of the sensorized hammerstone.

Pressure_Force_Data_prototype.txt – pressure signal and corresponding applied force data used for calibration of the prototype sensorized hammerstone.
Pressure_Force_model_pilot_experiment_AS.txt – pressure–force data obtained during the anvil-supported (AS) pilot experiment.
Pressure_Force_model_pilot_experiment_FH.txt – pressure–force data obtained during the freehand (FH) pilot experiment.
Pressure_Force_model_steel_hammerstone.txt – pressure–force calibration data for the steel sensorized hammerstone.
Force_mass_pilot_experiment_AS.txt – percussion force and detached flake mass data from the anvil-supported (AS) pilot experiment.
Force_mass_pilot_experiment_FH.txt – percussion force and detached flake mass data from the freehand (FH) pilot experiment.

Force is reported in newtons (N), pressure sensor output in volts (V), and detached flake mass in grams (g).

Chapter 4 – Percussion force for flake detachment in freehand and anvil-supported knapping

This folder contains the experimental data used for the analyses presented in Chapter 4, together with the pressure–force calibration data used to convert the sensorized hammerstone signal into percussion force.

Data_chapter4.csv – analysis-ready dataset containing the experimental variables used for the statistical analyses presented in Chapter 4.
Pressure_Force_model_P1.txt – pressure–force calibration data associated with the hammerstone used by Participant 1.
Pressure_Force_model_P2.txt – pressure–force calibration data associated with the hammerstone used by Participant 2.

The Chapter 4 dataset contains data from freehand (FH) and anvil-supported (AS) knapping trials. Variables include measurements associated with percussion mechanics and resulting flake characteristics.

Chapter 5 – Pre-strike wrist kinematics and hammerstone impact mechanics are influenced by core support conditions in knapping

This folder contains the experimental data used for the biomechanical and mechanical analyses presented in Chapter 5 and the pressure–force calibration data used for the sensorized hammerstone.

Data_chapter5.csv – analysis-ready dataset containing the experimental variables used for the statistical analyses presented in Chapter 5.
Pressure_Force_model_chapter5.txt – pressure–force calibration data used to convert the sensorized hammerstone signal into percussion force for the Chapter 5 experiment.

The Chapter 5 dataset contains data from the different core-support conditions examined in the experiment, including freehand and anvil-supported conditions with different supporting materials. The dataset includes variables related to pre-strike kinematics, percussion mechanics, and resulting flake characteristics.
